SplitChoc64 STAGE278 - XIAO nRF52840 Plus 3D mechanical model

Purpose:
- Enclosure / case clearance review.
- USB-C connector overhang verification.

Dimensions taken from the existing U1/U2 footprint:
- XIAO module PCB body: approximately 20.950 x 17.800 mm
- USB-C envelope: 6.350 x 7.620 mm
- USB-C protrusion beyond XIAO PCB edge: 1.515 mm

Important:
This STEP is a mechanical proxy. The PCB body and USB-C envelope follow the
existing footprint geometry. Small components are representative shapes.
It is not claimed to be an official Seeed Studio XIAO nRF52840 Plus STEP model.

For case design, the USB-C opening should not be made exactly 1.515 mm deep;
provide assembly/printing tolerance around the connector.
