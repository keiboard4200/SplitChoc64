HAND-SOLDERING RISKS
1. JST SH BM02B-SRSS-TB: 1.0mm-pitch SMD; use flux, magnification and a fine tip/hot air.
2. 0603 LED/resistor: small; LED polarity must be checked.
3. 64x 1N4148W: cathode orientation must be checked before soldering.
4. XIAO Plus BAT_SW/GND bottom pads: use the designed plated access slots; solder method needs care.
5. PCM12SMTR: orientation-sensitive and obsolete; buy spare units because the footprint is exact.
6. Choc hot-swap sockets: plastic can deform from excessive iron dwell time.
7. Battery/JST polarity: verify with a multimeter before connecting a LiPo.
8. Joystick is not a direct PCB footprint here; J3/J4 are 1.27mm 1x7 headers, so wiring/adapter is needed.
