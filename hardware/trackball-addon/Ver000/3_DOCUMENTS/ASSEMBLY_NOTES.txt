SplitChoc64 Trackball Add-on assembly notes

Signal path:
SplitChoc64 RIGHT J4 -> J1 on adapter PCB -> J2 (B-side) -> 6P 0.5 mm FFC -> PAW3222 14 mm sensor module.

Adapter J2 pin map:
1 GND
2 SCLK
3 SDIO
4 MOTION
5 CS
6 3V3

Sensor module connector pin map:
1 3V3
2 CS
3 MOTION
4 SDIO
5 SCLK
6 GND

With the intended connector facing arrangement and same-contact-side FFC, the physical conductor mapping is adapter 1..6 -> sensor 6..1.
The FFC is U-folded for routing; it is not twisted.
